About Us

  • Registered as an organization under Section 7 of the Societies Act 1966.
  • Registration No. PPM-005-18102016.

Objectives

Provide knowledge and information to the community about the harms of alcohol

Act as an advocacy group for the community to control alcohol misuse

Act as an intervention and support group for individuals, families, and communities experiencing problems related to alcohol misuse

What is mindfulness?

  • The ability to bring oneself and one's thoughts back to the present moment
  • Teaches the ability to think more:
    • Flexible
    • Open to new experiences
    • More compassionate towards oneself
    • Explore one's inner experiences
  • Usually involves breathing exercises
  • Can also be done in other ways
